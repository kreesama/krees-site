HipHop Choreo · Friday 20:30 · 22:00

A clear, musical choreography that feels good to dance and to watch.

I use choreography as a tool to fix foundations, train musicality and sharpen intention. You leave with cleaner moves, a better read of the track and tools you can reuse in your freestyle.

Friday class

20:30 · 22:00 at Centre de Danse du Marais, studio Mozart, 41 rue du Temple, Paris. We build one choreography over two to three sessions, with music, clarity and enjoyment at the centre.

Location

Centre de Danse du Marais, studio Mozart. Central studio, wooden floor, clean sound system. Easy metro access and many spots around if you want to debrief after class.

Class cards

5-class card · 80 € valid 6 weeks. 10-class card · 150 € valid 3 months. Usable on Fridays and on selected Saturday HipHopLabs.

Practical info

Comfortable outfit, clean sneakers, water. Cash payment on site. Cancellation possible up to 48 h before class, after that the session is due.

Class mindset

Clear work, humour, kindness. Space for trying, space for failing. The goal is to progress together without judgement, while building precision and presence.

HipHopLab · Saturday 10:00 · 12:00

Freestyle lab · small group · guided research.

The Lab is where we dig. We work with guided autonomy around clear themes, to help you improvise from your own dance. Small groups, detailed feedback, time to actually experiment.

Saturday sessions

Saturday morning, 10:00 · 12:00. Dates are announced throughout the season. Each Lab has one main theme and room for open exploration.

Location

Around Bibliothèque nationale François Mitterrand (BNF) in Paris : outdoor whenever possible, sometimes indoors. The exact spot is confirmed the day before on Instagram or WhatsApp.

Class cards

Same cards as HipHop Choreo. You can mix Friday class and Saturday Labs depending on what you need and your schedule.

Practical info

Limited spots for real feedback. Loose outfit, sneakers, water. Filming is allowed at the end so you can keep track of your progress.

Approach

We clarify freestyle fundamentals : musical listening, directions, transitions, levels, gaze. I give you simple, precise tasks you can later rework on your own.

Research themes · non-exhaustive examples

Musical reading & inner groove

Spot the layers of the track, find your own bounce, play with silence and breath.

Textures, weight, body transformation

Switch from soft to sharp, from heavy to elastic, while keeping a clear intention.

Transitions & connectors

Link your basics without breaking, manage level, direction and rhythm changes.

Directions & levels

Really dance in 360°, play with height, design paths so the audience can follow.

Solo freestyle & readability

Build an entry, a middle and an exit. Hold one simple idea and own it.

Duos, trios, collective awareness

Listen to others, manage roles and mirrors, without losing your own presence.

Events

Artist support

Singers, dancers, actors, creators, non-dancers.

My job here is to make things easier. I help you put your body in service of what you want to say, without erasing your identity or your natural way of moving.

Presence coaching

Posture, gaze, stage fright, speaking, relationship to mic, stage or camera. We work from your natural energy, not from a fake character.

Movement direction

For music videos, live shows, shoots, campaigns. We build a movement vocabulary that fits your universe, guide dancers and non-dancers, and organise space and dynamics.

Choreo for clips & live

Tailor-made choreography, with or without dancers. I can bring in other specialists depending on the style you want. We think energy, musicality and clarity on stage and on camera.

About

I’m Krees. Dancer, choreographer and movement director, rooted in hip hop freestyle. I’ve been working on French, Belgian and international stages for more than fifteen years. I’m currently based in Brussels and I move between Paris, Belgium and Europe, switching between stages, studios, labs and artist coaching.

I’ve danced, created and taught in many different contexts, from battles to brands, from theatres to festivals. In all these spaces, I keep the same priorities : music first, clear movement, and an honest aesthetic that starts from the person in front of me.
