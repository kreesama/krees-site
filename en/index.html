About

Based in Brussels, working frequently in Paris and travelling across Europe. Dancer, choreographer and movement director rooted in hip-hop freestyle. The aim is clarity: readable, honest, efficient movement for stage and image. Since 2010 I teach, coach and support artists on musicality, movement quality, transitions and presence.

References include H&M, Adidas, Jordan, McDonald’s, Galeries Lafayette, ShelterSuit, Sweet Pants and J&B. Same approach every time: start from the person, build robust tools, seek the right expressivity.
