About Krees Sama

Christophe “Krees Sama” Jallerat is a professional hip-hop dancer, choreographer and movement coach based between Paris and Brussels. Born in Djibouti in 1986 to a French father and Ethiopian mother, he discovered hip-hop dance at 15 while living in Martinique, with a focus on popping. Over the years his style evolved, and about five years later he specialized in freestyle hip-hop:contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}:contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}. Drawn to improvisation, he took part in numerous dance battles and joined renowned crews (FORZESOUND, SECTION C). He eventually created his own group, Division For One, and danced with the ELAM company (Léa Djyl), solidifying his roots in hip-hop culture.

Krees has been teaching regularly since 2010:contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}, sharing his passion through weekly classes. With over a decade of experience, he began developing his own training methodology in 2020 to support dancers of all levels. His teaching approach is both humane and rigorous: he fosters a supportive environment that encourages learning through trial and error, while maintaining high standards to drive progress.

Throughout his career, Krees has worked with many brands and artists. Clients (dance/choreography): H&M, Adidas, McDonald’s, Jordan, etc. Artistic direction collaborations: Sweet Pants, among other creative projects (with no overlap between lists).

Hip-Hop Lab

The Hip-Hop Labs are monthly sessions led by Krees at the French National Library (BNF) in Paris. They serve as a creative laboratory where dancers can deeply explore freestyle movement under Krees’s guidance. Each Lab centers on a specific theme designed to develop autonomy, improvisation skills, and self-expression in dance. It’s a safe experimental space for pushing artistic boundaries and learning new approaches alongside Krees and fellow dancers.

Upcoming Hip-Hop Lab sessions:

  • Saturday, December 6, 2025 (2pm–5pm) – Theme: Musicality & Groove
  • Saturday, January 10, 2026 (2pm–5pm) – Theme: Expression & Emotions
  • Saturday, February 7, 2026 (2pm–5pm) – Theme: Creativity & Autonomy

Labs take place outdoors on the BNF esplanade and are open to dancers eager to enrich their personal style. Each session has limited spots to ensure quality guidance and interaction.

Workshops

Krees regularly offers intensive workshops in France and abroad to share his passion and expertise with dancers looking to level up their skills. As a guest instructor at various dance camps and schools, he provides participants the opportunity to train in focused, immersive sessions. These premium workshops are a chance to learn from a top choreographer in an environment of collective motivation and artistic exploration.

Freestyle Workshop

An intensive workshop dedicated to freestyle and improvisation. In this session, Krees emphasizes on-the-spot creativity: techniques to approach battles, musicality exercises, and developing your personal style in cyphers. It’s ideal for gaining confidence in improvisation and boosting your battle skills, all with tips from an experienced competitor.

Methodology Workshop

A workshop focused on Krees’s training methodology. Through innovative and progressive exercises, he shares his approach to improving technique, musicality, and creativity. Participants discover new tools to refine their dance and deepen their understanding of movement. This workshop is perfect for dancers who want a structured training experience and to reach the next level in their practice.

Throughout the year, other themed workshops are organized (choreography, dancer personal development, etc.), including events in Brussels to engage with the Belgian dance community. Upcoming workshop dates are announced on Instagram and via newsletter.

Artistic Coaching

As an artistic coach, Krees guides dancers and artists in achieving their goals. His tailor-made coaching adapts to each individual’s needs, whether it’s improving dance skills or bringing a stage project to life. With a keen eye and a supportive approach, Krees helps unlock the full artistic potential of every person he works with.

Personal Coaching

One-on-one, personalized training for dancers who want to accelerate their progress. From technique and musicality to stage presence, Krees develops a custom program to address key areas and develop your unique style. Whether you’re preparing for an audition, a competition, or simply aiming to gain confidence, you’ll benefit from dedicated coaching that pushes you toward excellence.

Artistic Direction

Krees also serves as a movement director and artistic consultant on various professional projects: music videos, stage shows, commercials, and events. Backed by his collaborations with major brands, he brings a creative vision to craft impactful choreography that aligns with the project’s identity. From initial concept to final performance, his expertise ensures solid artistic direction every step of the way.
